HAND-CUT EGG NOODLES 🔪 No fancy tools or equipment needed, just your hands and a handful of ingredients. You can add these noodles to soup, stir-fry them or serve with a simple sauce.
Noodle Dough
- 500g plain flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 6 eggs
- Cornstarch, for dusting
Hot Oil Sauce (optional)
- 2 tsp minced garlic
- 2 tsp minced spring onion
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp black vinegar
- 1/2 tbsp dark soy sauce
Mix flour and salt together on a clean work surface. Make a well in the centre then add in the eggs. Using chopsticks gently break up the eggs, then using your hands working the dough until a shaggy ball. Don’t worry if it gets messy, it will all come together. Knead for 8 - 10 minutes until smooth then wrap in cling wrap and rest for 30 minutes. Cut into 4 portions and working with 1 portion, roll to your desired thickness. Make sure to use cornstarch to dust. Fold the dough onto itself then cut to your desired thickness. To cook the noodles, bring a large pot of water to a boil and cook for 60-90 seconds. Drain, and use as desired.
To make the hot oil sauce, place into a mound the minced garlic and spring onion, chilli flakes and white sesame seeds. Heat oil until hot then pour over to sizzle. Add light soy sauce, black vinegar and dark soy sauce. Toss until well combined and enjoy.
