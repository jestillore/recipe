## Chicken Fillet Ala-King

### Batter
- 450g chicken breast
- 120g all purpose flour
- 60g corn starch
- 2 cups breadcrumbs
- 1/2 lemon juice or 3 calamansi
- 1 cup water
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 3 cloves minced garlic
- 2 eggs

### Sauce
- 60g butter
- 1/2 cup chopped carrots
- 1pc chicken cube
- 60g all purpose flour
- 1 cup water
- 370mL evaporated milk
- 250mL cream
